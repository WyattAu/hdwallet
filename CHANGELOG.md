## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-01

### Added

- BIP39 mnemonic generation (24-word phrases) and seed derivation.
- BIP44 derivation path support per coin.
- Multi-chain address/keys: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Tron.
- Pure Rust, `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`.
